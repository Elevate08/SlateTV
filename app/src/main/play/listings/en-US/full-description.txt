Slate TV is a minimal, gallery-first launcher for Android TV. A full-screen photo backdrop sets the scene, with a clock, photo captions, and a dock for your favorite apps. Open All Apps to find the rest, or let the home screen fade into an idle gallery.

Slate TV is engineered for fast, reliable wake recovery on Android TV, handling HDMI-CEC display and HDR mode switches cleanly without blank screens. No Accessibility Service is required.

Open AIDL interfaces let independently installable companion plugins add custom background photo sources and optional home shelves such as live TV and program guides. Companion plugins run in separate apps and stay hidden whenever unconfigured or unavailable, keeping your home screen uncluttered.

Slate TV is free, with zero ads and zero paywalls. The optional Tip Jar supports development. A tip unlocks nothing, and every launcher feature is available without a purchase.
